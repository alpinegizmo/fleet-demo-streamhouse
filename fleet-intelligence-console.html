Safety recommendations

Heuristic vs. per-vehicle baseline

Same raw telemetry, two ways of flagging a driver-risk event: driver.risk.events uses a fixed threshold (speed > 95 km/h or a harsh-braking score > 0.7) applied identically to every vehicle. driver.risk.events.anomaly replaces the speed half of that with a per-vehicle z-score in Flink SQL: each reading is compared with the mean and standard deviation of that vehicle's own previous 30 speeds, and flagged only when it's more than 3 standard deviations away. The comparison below only covers the overspeed half of the heuristic - the baseline job only watches speed, so harsh-braking alerts (0 in this window) aren't something it can confirm or refute, and every card in both feeds below shows its own verdict rather than just a total.
